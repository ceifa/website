title: "The trap wave"
written: "2020-07-23"
---

Just like there were times when everyone wanted to be a rock star or a football player, we're living in one where everyone wants to make trap. The number of people I know doing it is getting out of hand. The pattern is obvious: they mirror what they like and go after it to try to do the same. Kids from the outskirts, "rich" kids with stable jobs, it doesn't matter. Sooner or later they'll send you their new track and tell you their new name is lil X or yung Y.

## Originality

The truth I see behind all this is that it barely matters whether you have a good sound or a good producer. In the end it's all about having connections and, even more, originality.

Sidoka, Recayd, Matuê, they all brought something new to the scene. And Cristal, I can't even put into words that much genius in someone so young, who has been writing poetry since childhood with a mix of depth and abstraction that became a signature. [Have a listen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJw_3bTnCfI).

## Trap's golden age hasn't happened yet

This is the best time to live trap. While the US already has a defined scene with big names, Brazil is still building its own. There's still a lot of room for new artists, and society's growing acceptance is making that possible. 2019 was a great year for the scene, but I don't think we've lived the big era yet. Trap is still on track to be the next big thing, the same way rock and funk were in past decades.
